# Official and public leases

Public UK lease material assembled for the hack. Nothing here is an executed lease on a real building.

## Model Commercial Lease (free templates)

Download the current versions from the official site: https://www.modelcommerciallease.co.uk/downloads/

Conditions of Use (read these): https://www.modelcommerciallease.co.uk/conditions-of-use/

You may download, store and reproduce the templates, free of charge, at your own risk. Copies in this folder were fetched on 12 November 2026 so you can work if the site is slow. Prefer a fresh official download if you need the very latest text.

We saved current versions of: Office 01 and 02, Retail 01, 02 and 07, Logistic 01, Food/Drink 01, Agreement for lease AFL 01, Deposit 01, Lease Clauses 01, 03, 05 and 06, Licence to assign 01, and Licence for alterations 01.

## Land Registry prescribed clauses (LR1–LR14)

Most registrable leases in England and Wales granted on or after 19 June 2006 must open with these clauses.

- Practice Guide 64: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/prescribed-clauses-leases/practice-guide-64-prescribed-clauses-leases
- Table of clauses: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/leases-prescribed-clauses-table
- Legal wording, Schedule 1A: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1417/schedule/1A
- Licence: Open Government Licence v3.0
